# 38. Document continuity and future decision governance

This is the **canonical gameplay bible** for *Sovereigns*. Its world, politics, territorial control, passage rights, economy, warfare and battles form one integrated intended design.

## 38.1 Evolving the living document

Material design changes are made by editing the authoritative rule in place, reconciling upstream and downstream chapters, updating relevant examples and tests, and recording a dated decision in a separate version history or in an added future-version entry. Obsolete rules must not remain in the normative body merely because they were once discussed. An agent must not treat illustrative figures as permanent constants or silent authority to reduce scope.

## 38.2 Required companion documents

- `SOVEREIGNS_VISUAL_DIRECTION.md`: visual identity, camera choices within 2D orthographic presentation, art assets, animation, UI appearance and audiovisual language. Style is deliberately **not** decided here.
- Engineering architecture and implementation specifications: technology, interfaces, code, data schemas, persistence design, tasks and deployment. They implement this bible; they do not redefine game behavior.
- Balance and content catalogs: commodity definitions, troop families, crop thresholds, institution varieties and quantitative tuning. Such files remain subordinate to the system contracts here.

## 38.3 Change discipline

Every gameplay amendment must answer: what player-facing rule changes, what physical/political/economic state it affects, how it crosses campaign and tactical boundaries, what AI must do, what information appears in the UI, what happens during save/load, and which reproducible acceptance scenario proves it. An apparently easier implementation that contradicts an invariant is a design defect, not a permitted shortcut.

## 38.4 Living-version record

**1.1.0 · 2026-10-08:** Physical-field atlas and causal environmental simulation defined as the game's authoritative foundation. Geology, terrain, weather, solar/seasonal energy, moisture, atmosphere, hydrology, lakes, snow, soils and ecosystems now have explicit data dependencies and gameplay contracts across the rest of the bible. Includes traceable cross-system scenarios and physical acceptance tests. Earlier edition retained separately for archival reference; the present file contains the complete normative rules.

## Final creative north star

*Sovereigns* is a living, geographically grounded medieval world seen through strategic governance and detailed tactical battles. Its geography forms ecosystems, economies and civilizations. Its kings govern imperfectly, through institutions and relationships rather than universal magical ownership. Armies travel, fight, negotiate, threaten, occupy and blockade real places. Control follows access, force and cooperation; legitimate borders follow politics. Wars are won through decisions about terrain, people, resources and time—not painting loops on a map. Battles matter because their locations, conditions and consequences are part of the same persistent world.
