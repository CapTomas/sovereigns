# 37. Open-ended balance, without open design holes

The **design choices are made**; numerical calibration remains empirical. Agent teams should not regard missing exact values as missing rules. The following are tunable families, all governed by the sections above:

- Seasonal rainfall and temperature variance, storm incidence, mountain snow persistence.
- Soil classes, crop thresholds, harvest yield scales and seed/storage requirements.
- Population consumption and labor participation rates; migration sensitivity.
- Market adjustment speed, transport loading costs, carrying capacity and risk.
- Tax bands, obligations, debt terms and administrative collection losses.
- Regiment sizes, armor/equipment weights, training costs, ammunition and morale responses.
- Army baggage capacity, sustained marching distance, fatigue recovery and logistics throughput.
- Battle frontage, formation spacing, projectile ballistics and contact loss rates.
- Political loyalty, institution influence and AI risk tolerance.
- World generation parameters, region size, detail resolution and simulation update schedules.

All tuning should be done against reproducible scenarios, historical plausibility where relevant, legibility, fun and performance. It must not break conservation or create disconnected arbitrary bonuses. If a number materially changes the gameplay model, it ceases to be “just balance” and requires a design amendment.
