# 28. Core game settings, pacing and quality-of-life rules

## 28.1 Campaign creation settings

Editable world seed, geographic profile, continent scale, broad climate diversity, length/depth of generated human history, geopolitical maturity, AI challenge and optional campaign ambition. Defaults produce a geographically varied continent with functional civilizations and opportunities for a medium-sized regional kingdom. Settings must produce legible warnings if chosen combination yields extreme worlds.

**Excluded as standard game modes:** fixed map of historical Europe, fantasy spellcasting, globally symmetric competitive map, fully character-driven dynasty game. These cannot be switched on by a miscellaneous checkbox without rewriting fundamental world assumptions.

## 28.2 Campaign controls

Pause/planning stage, end turn, auto-advance, standing orders, pinned objectives, filters, path previews, multi-army selection where appropriate, commander assignment, project queues and advisers. Default stop conditions cover battles, sieges, major economic crisis, treaty deadlines and threat to core territory. Repeated routine reports can be summarized by season.

## 28.3 Battle controls and speed

Pause, multiple simulation speeds where performance permits, grouped formations, queue orders, save/load, battle information overlays and camera bookmarks. Sim speed affects **simulation scheduling only**; it does not change physical outcomes beyond documented numerical limitations. Adjustable AI tactical assistance may coordinate subordinate regiments if chosen but must not remove player authority.

## 28.4 Modding/content extensibility

Game rules, crop species, commodity categories, equipment, unit roles, buildings, cultural names, government institutions and presentation assets should be extensible through content data. Expose stable semantics and validation rather than promising every underlying equation is user-scriptable. Mods cannot rely on arbitrary resource duplication or uncoupled bonuses without explicitly replacing core rules in their own game mode. The official balance always follows the conservation and persistence principles.

## 28.5 Language and localization

Design content identifiers and generated naming consistently to permit localization of UI, rules and chronicle templates. Generated proper nouns should be transliterable/pronounceable and map labels should handle variable lengths. Dates, units and numeral display follow localization without changing simulation values.
