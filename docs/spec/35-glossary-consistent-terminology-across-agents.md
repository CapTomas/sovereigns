# 35. Glossary: consistent terminology across agents

**Atmospheric humidity:** Water vapor condition of air; relative humidity and actual vapor quantity are not interchangeable.  
**Atmospheric advection:** Transport of air, heat and atmospheric water by moving wind fields.  
**Catchment / watershed:** Area draining by connected paths toward a channel, lake or terminal outlet.  
**Coupled simulation:** Process where outputs of one physical system become state/inputs of another and can feed back over time.  
**Environmental snapshot:** Consistent set of physical conditions queried at a world coordinate and authoritative time.  
**Field atlas:** Authoritative multi-resolution physical representation storing/querying spatial and temporal environmental state.  
**Groundwater:** Subsurface water storage/recharge/discharge model, distinct from surface soil wetness.  
**Hydrological discharge:** Water volume flowing through a river reach over time; distinct from channel width or painted river classification.  
**Infiltration:** Transfer of precipitation or surface water into the soil, constrained by material and current saturation.  
**Microclimate:** Terrain- and land-cover-derived local variation within larger atmospheric conditions.  
**Orographic precipitation:** Rain/snow generated or enhanced when moist air is lifted by terrain and cools.  
**Physical provenance:** Explanation chain connecting an observed outcome to its stored physical fields and events.  
**Relative humidity:** Current vapor relative to the saturation capacity at that temperature, not moisture stored in land.  
**Runoff:** Water moving over or near the land surface toward channels and storage after local infiltration/retention.  
**Snow water equivalent:** Stored water amount represented by snow; determines eventual meltwater contribution.  
**Soil moisture:** Water stored in soil layers, governing plants, infiltration and ground condition; not atmospheric humidity.  
**World field:** Stored, evolving or derived spatial property belonging to the authoritative physical simulation.  
**Administrative region:** Governable collection of territory and subjects; a political/economic unit, not a physics cell.  
**Army:** Operational organization of regiments, commander, supplies and baggage, moving through the campaign world.  
**Authoritative world:** Shared truth of geography, time, people, goods, rights, infrastructure and events.  
**Autoresolve:** Lower-cost battle outcome method respecting the same physical and military inputs as manual battle.  
**Biome:** Ecological pattern derived from climate, soil, water and species conditions, not decorative paint alone.  
**Battle context:** Exact engagement place, time, armies, orders, terrain, weather, preparation and intelligence passed into tactical simulation.  
**Campaign turn:** Default seven-day planning/resolution period composed of smaller chronological steps.  
**Claim:** Asserted political/legal right to territory; distinct from administration or military occupation.  
**Climate:** Long-run seasonal patterns, probabilities and geography-dependent conditions.  
**Cohort:** Aggregate population group with demographic, occupation, wealth, culture and legal attributes.  
**Cohesion:** Degree of formation organization and ability to maneuver/fight as ordered.  
**Commodity:** Physical good with quantity, location, ownership and economic uses.  
**Culture:** Historical social/linguistic identity and learned traditions across populations, not identical to state or geography.  
**Derived field:** Value calculated from authoritative physical/economic inputs, not an independent arbitrary modifier.  
**Domain:** Royal or institutional assets under direct economic control.  
**Drainage network:** Connected system of downhill flow and accumulation through streams, rivers, lakes and outlets.  
**Engagement:** Physical encounter between hostile forces at a time and location under operational rules.  
**Fortification:** Persistent defense geometry, stores and institutions occupying a world location.  
**Fog of war:** Faction's imperfect knowledge of true state, observation and reported information.  
**Formation:** Spatial arrangement, frontage, depth, spacing and facing of a regiment.  
**Genesis:** Procedural formation of geography, ecology, settlement, societies and historical starting state.  
**Ground condition:** Wetness, firmness, snow/ice, cover and slope controlling actual movement/combat.  
**Holding:** Land, site or productive asset with legally defined ownership/use/jurisdiction rights.  
**Institution:** Durable political, economic, religious or civic organization with rights, resources and members.  
**Inventory:** Owned physical stock located in a warehouse, household, market, project, convoy or army.  
**Jurisdiction:** Recognized ability to administer/enforce law in an area; distinct from ownership of all goods.  
**Land parcel:** Spatial management unit for land use, ownership and agricultural/ecological activity.  
**Local market:** Trading area with available goods, buyers, sellers, prices and connection to transport routes.  
**Logistics:** Movement and allocation of supplies, animals, equipment and other support to armies/projects.  
**Morale:** Willingness of troops to remain and obey amid danger; distinct from cohesion and fatigue.  
**Political control:** Actual authority that can be exercised; may differ from claims or legal sovereignty.  
**Procedural history:** Past state-changing events simulated during genesis, forming current conditions and chronicle.  
**Regiment:** Primary selectable and commanded tactical military unit, composed of soldiers and equipment.  
**Royal treasury:** Spendable crown funds, not an aggregate of all citizens' wealth.  
**Shipment:** Goods physically in transit with origin/destination, quantity and delivery conditions.  
**Sovereign polity:** Political entity controlled by player; can survive rulers, dynasties and negotiated status changes.  
**Tactical refinement:** Deterministic higher-resolution representation of actual world coordinates for battle.  
**Trade edge:** Specific road, river, maritime or other link with real cost, throughput and condition.  
**Vassal:** Subordinate political authority bound by defined obligations and privileges, not a free crown asset.  
**Weather:** Time-varying local atmospheric state that influences terrain, crops, transport and battles.  
**World seed:** Input used to generate reproducible starting world, not a substitute for evolved save state.

**Access rights:** Treaty-, law- and conflict-dependent permission to cross, trade, supply, patrol, garrison or stage from territory; distinct from friendship and sovereignty.  
**Administrative jurisdiction:** Authority to administer law, collect specified dues and govern institutions, not identical to ownership or occupation.  
**Blockade / encirclement:** Restriction of physically viable transport and movement connections around a force or location; not an arbitrary filled polygon.  
**Contested territory:** Ground where no authority can continuously enforce local military decisions due to rival power, resistance or insecure routes.  
**Control corridor:** Maintained route or region secured by accessible forces, patrols, settlements and supplies, not just a marching trail.  
**Military access treaty:** Scoped right of passage and possibly supply/basing, which does not imply territorial annexation.  
**Military control:** Practical local enforcement capacity from actual forces and routes; may be secured, contested or threatened and changes over time.  
**Occupation:** Military enforcement over specific sites/areas requiring durable capacity and local responses, without automatically transferring legal sovereignty.  
**Secured location:** A site with credible continuing protection and local enforcement, supported by appropriate garrisons/patrols and access.  
**Threatened location:** Ground within plausible hostile operational reach or under raid but not sustainably occupied.
