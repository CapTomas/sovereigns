## SOV-P02-T01 — World ID, seed, generation version, immutable coordinate contract
`WorldIdentity` holds the world ID, seed, scenario, rules version, generator version, extent, geographic reference, step and start. It is immutable by construction: a positional record with no mutating API. `SaveTests` shows it unchanged across save and load, and saves refuse generator versions this build cannot regenerate.

- **World ID:** 128-bit, created by the host from operating-system randomness, saved, and excluded from checksums (`WorldTests.ChecksumDependsOnSeedTimeAndCommandsButNotWorldId`).
- **Geographic reference:** φ = φ₀ + y/R; local mean solar time from distance east of the standard meridian; ±85° and meridian checks (`GeographyTests`).

ADR-0006. **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T02 — Calendar, days, seasons, hours, local solar time, chronology
`WorldCalendar` follows ADR-0006:

- 365 days, Gregorian common-year months, no leap days;
- epoch 0001-01-01T00:00 world standard time, with astronomical year numbering for negative times;
- meteorological seasons, mirrored by hemisphere, lasting 12.9–13.1 seven-day turns (spec §2.3);
- canonical ISO-like text.

`CalendarTests` (16 cases) covers:
- round trips, including year 0 and negative years;
- no 29 February, and invalid dates;
- season boundaries, and season lengths counted from the calendar;
- a world advancing from year −1 through year 0 into year 1 with `YearBegan` events. Local solar time is in `GeographyTests`, and the client shows it in the inspector. **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T03 — One authoritative clock independent of the frame rate
`World.Now` changes only through `AdvanceTo` and `Step`. Hosts convert real time with `RealTimePacer`, and rendering never touches the clock. `DecisionTests.PacerFollowsSpeedAndPauseAndStepRequests` covers this, and so does the client test of frame-pattern invariance (T16). **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T04 — Scheduler priorities, cadence and time-step ownership
`ISimulationSystem` declares priority, cadence, phase and owned components. The scheduler is event-driven and runs due systems by priority, then name, when the clock leaves an instant. The priority table follows spec §29.9 (ADR-0007). The calendar is the first real system (daily, priority 0). `SchedulerTests` covers:

- order;
- phase;
- invalid cadences;
- duplicate owners;
- commands at an instant preceding that instant's updates;
- one-call, daily and irregular advancement over 400 days giving identical ledgers, with exactly one season or year event per boundary (spec §30.2).

**State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T05 — Base, mutable, derived and observed state separated
ADR-0007 "State classes":

- **Generated base state:** the immutable `WorldIdentity`.
- **Mutable authoritative state:** the clock, IDs, ledger, markers, breakpoints and decisions, each with an owner.
- **Derived values:** inspections, calendar, latitude and solar time, computed and never saved.
- **Cached values:** `DerivedCache` with declared sources.
- **Observed information:** a separate contract (Phase 25). The developer inspector is marked as an omniscient view.

**Deviation:** the observed-information class is a contract only until factions exist (Phase 25).

`SaveFile` persists only identity, mutable state and the ledger (ADR-0008). **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T06 — Deterministic random streams keyed by system, place, event and seed
`RandomStream` is counter-based: SplitMix64 over FNV-1a purpose hashing and key parts, resumable from a position. Systems name purposes `<system>.<purpose>` (`SystemContext.Stream`). `RandomStreamTests` covers:

- golden values from [`tools/random_stream_reference.py`](../../tools/random_stream_reference.py), an independent implementation of ADR-0007's exact definition, pinned by both test suites;
- key sensitivity;
- independence from other streams' consumption;
- resume from a position;
- bounds and uniformity.

UI randomness cannot reach outcomes: the banned-API analyzer excludes `System.Random` from the simulation, and the client acts only through commands. No Phase 02 system draws randomness yet; Phase 04 genesis is the first consumer. **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T07 — Ordered world-event ledger
`EventLedger` of `WorldEvent` is append-only and chronological, with increasing `event:N` IDs. Each event has a type, a cause (origin, command sequence, system, or parent event), subjects and data. `KernelStateTests` checks that it refuses to go back in time and that every change is recorded with its cause. The client's events panel shows the newest 12. **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T08 — Stable IDs for features, settlements, facilities, armies, polities, tactical objects
`EntityId<TKind>` has typed kinds: feature, settlement, facility, army, regiment, polity, tactical, event, marker and breakpoint. `IdAllocator` keeps per-kind monotonic counters; IDs are never reused, 0 means none, and counters are saved. `KernelStateTests` covers per-kind independence and serialization; `WorldTests` covers markers never being reused. **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T09 — Write boundaries and explicit invalidation
`OwnershipRegistry` allows one owner per component, and systems declare `OwnedComponents`. A duplicate owner fails when it registers.

Writes are guarded by encapsulation:
- state has no public setters;
- ID allocation is internal since the review;
- hosts write only through commands;
- systems get only their `SystemContext`.

`StateVersion` stamps and `DerivedCache` declare sources and recompute only after a source changes. **Scope today:** one `StateVersion` (markers) and one cache, the marker spatial index. 50 queries make 1 computation, and it recomputes after a marker change (`KernelStateTests`). Later phases add a version per cached component (ADR-0007). **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T10 — Spatial query semantics for unloaded, loading, loaded and refined areas
ADR-0007 "Spatial query semantics" requires results independent of residency, with residency affecting only cost, and specifies how each state answers. Queries are read-only: `KernelStateTests.QueriesNeverChangeStateWhateverTheView` interleaves inspections at in-world, distant and NaN points and finds an unchanged checksum. **Deviation:** chunk machinery is SOV-P03-T07, so in Phase 02 every area answers from base state; the contract is defined and tested only through query side effects. **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T11 — Pause, step, accelerated stepping, non-skippable decisions; shell time controls through commands
**Kernel:**
- `World.Control` handles `Pause`, `Resume`, `SetSpeed` (presets of 1 s, 1 min, 1 h, 1 day and 1 week per real second) and `StepOnce`, and `RealTimePacer` turns real time into steps.
- `SetBreakpoint` and `AcknowledgeDecision` are journaled world commands. A due decision stops `AdvanceTo` at any target or speed until acknowledged.
- Step requests are refused while a decision is pending and are cleared when one is raised (a worker finding, fixed in the kernel).

`DecisionTests` covers stopping, simultaneous decisions, 1 week/s, pacing, backlog cap and step requests.

**Shell:**
- Space pauses, `.` steps, keys 1–5 set speed, B sets a breakpoint at the next midnight, and Enter acknowledges.
- The HUD shows date, season and time state, and a decision banner appears.
- Client tests: `TimeControlTests` and `ShellTests`.

**Design change during the phase:** control commands were first journaled. That consumed command sequence numbers, so pausing changed later events' causes and the checksum. They now bypass the journal, so pacing is host state only (ADR-0007). **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T12 — Delta/event persistence primitives without the client
`SaveFile` holds the identity, mutable state and ledger (the deltas). The headless runner writes `--save` and continues with `--load`; journal sequences continue across a save. A failure report from a loaded session carries the save's hash, and `replay --save` reproduces it. The client has no save code. Tests:

- `SaveTests.SaveLoadContinueEqualsAnUninterruptedRun`, run with a pending decision;
- `DeterminismTests`, which saves at a random day for five seeds;
- `HeadlessTests.SaveAndLoadContinueTheFixtureToTheSameState` (F-KERNEL day 100);
- `FailureAfterLoadingReplaysFromThatSave`.

**State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T13 — Field-value provenance diagnostics
Each `FieldReading` is an SI number with basis (query, stored, derived, estimated or unavailable), owner, source, derivation and update time. Latitude and local mean solar time are now available as derived readings; elevation is unavailable until Phase 04. The inspector shows provenance lines (capture `p02-muster.png`, client test (f)). `WorldTests.InspectionReportsEveryFieldFamilyWithProvenance…` covers it. **State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T14 — Assertions for temporal order, missing IDs, duplicate owners
- **Temporal order:**
  - `AdvanceTo` refuses past or off-grid targets;
  - the ledger refuses older events and non-increasing IDs;
  - breakpoints must be in the future;
  - journals refuse out-of-order or gapped entries;
  - saves refuse ledgers past the save time.
- **Missing IDs:**
  - commands with unknown IDs are rejected with "does not exist" or "already removed";
  - save loading reports never-issued or duplicate IDs and missing set or raised events, and never invents them (`SaveTests.BrokenReferencesAreReportedNotInvented`).
- **Duplicate owners:** `DuplicateOwnerException` at registration (`SchedulerTests`).

**State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T15 — Same seed and command stream give identical checksums
`DeterminismTests` uses five seeds, including 0xFFFF…, and seeded command streams over 90 days that mix markers, removals, breakpoints, acknowledgements, invalid commands and pacing. A repeated run, a journal replay, and a save-load-continue at a random split all give identical checksums and journals; different seeds differ.

- **Fixture level:** `F-EMPTY` and `F-KERNEL` are run twice by `check_determinism.py` in CI on three operating systems.
- **Cross-OS:** the `determinism` job now compares both fixtures per operating system. ADR-0004 §3 records the bit-identical level, which is trivial until floating-point fields arrive.

**State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T16 — Pause, frame rates and view changes do not alter results
- **Client (worker test (e)):** `F-KERNEL` driven with 1/60 s frames, and with irregular 0.05–0.25 s frames plus hitches, pauses, single steps and speed changes, reaches identical checksums, ledgers and journals. Breaking the pacer's stream limit made this test fail, which shows it can fail.
- **Kernel:** `WorldTests.ControlCommandsChangePacingButNeitherStateNorJournal` and `SchedulerTests` (advancement chunking).
- **Views:** `KernelStateTests.QueriesNeverChangeStateWhateverTheView`.

**Deviation:** the "loaded/unloaded view changes" half proves only that queries have no side effects. Residency does not exist until SOV-P03-T07 (see T10), so this half is re-proved once chunks load and unload.

**State:** Ready for review.

## SOV-P02-T17 — Versioned save format policy and integrity checks
ADR-0008 sets the policy:

- format name and version, with migrations N → N+1;
- the rules version must match, or a migration must be declared;
- a SHA-256 of the canonical payload;
- reference validation;
- deterministic bytes;
- policy for later components.

`SaveTests` covers:
- header mismatches: format name, newer and older format, rules version;
- an edited payload, and an unregenerable generator version;
- unknown counter kinds, and causes naming unapplied commands;
- an out-of-order ledger, and broken references;
- identical bytes on re-save, and that a faulted world cannot be saved. **State:** Ready for review.
