## SOV-P03-T03 — Units, ranges, no-data, interpolation, precision and error budgets
Each `FieldDefinition` carries:

- an SI unit (ADR-0004), kind and class;
- native level, interpolation (bilinear or nearest) and refinement (interpolate or conserve);
- a valid range;
- a no-data meaning (why a value can be missing; never zero) and a zero meaning, such as calm air, dry soil or no precipitation reaching the ground (spec §3.5);
- a storage precision and error budget, such as "float32; ≤ 0.05 m";
- an owner, dependencies, the delivering phase and a save schema version.

Saves refuse out-of-range values (`SaveFile.Validate`). Painting validates radius and value. **Deviation:** contracts of later-phase fields are intended contracts; their phases may refine them by amending ADR-0009. **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T04 — Deterministic arbitrary-coordinate sampling of static fields
`FieldAtlas.Sample(field, point, level)` returns:

- the value;
- the basis and classification;
- the grid inputs with their weights and stored flags;
- the time its inputs last changed.

Native sampling is bilinear between cell centres, using only + − × ÷. Near the world's edge it clamps; outside the world the sample is unavailable. `AtlasTests` covers:

- `SamplingInterpolatesCellCentresAndReportsItsInputs`;
- `UnavailableFieldsAndOutsidePointsNeverInventValues`;
- `AreaSamplingMatchesPointSampling`, which shows that `SampleArea` (used by overlays) agrees exactly with point queries.

**Determinism across platforms:** the headless run summary gains `atlas_probe`, a SHA-256 of latitude, scratch and gradient samples on a 16 × 16 lattice at all four levels. `check_determinism.py` compares it twice per OS, and the `determinism` job compares it across Ubuntu, Windows and macOS for each fixture. `HeadlessTests.TheAtlasProbeRepeatsAndSeesPaintedFields` shows the probe repeats exactly and changes when `F-ATLAS` paints. **Deviation:** no physical field has a generator until Phase 04; sampling is exercised through analytic fields and the developer scratch field. **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T06 — Continuous features with durable identities
`MapFeature` holds:

Developer survey lines are drawn and removed by the journaled `DrawSurveyLine` and `RemoveSurveyLine` commands, with IDs `survey:N` that are never reused. Features are saved and checksummed. **Deviation:** rivers, banks, shorelines, ridgelines and roads come from their generators (Phases 04, 05 and 19); Phase 03 provides the geometry, identity, index and persistence they use. **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T07 — Spatial indexing, chunk loading and unloading, queries on unresident regions
`ChunkCache` is one least-recently-used cache with a byte budget (64 MiB by default). It holds native, derived, aggregate and refined chunks; refined chunks hold a conserve field's per-cell refinement terms. Between calls an area is unloaded, loaded or refined. ADR-0007's "loading" happens synchronously inside the query that needs it. A query on an unloaded area loads what it needs. Chunks uniform in base and without writes are recognized without materializing them. Aggregation visits native chunks one at a time, so memory stays near the budget whatever the world's size (T16).

Tests:

- `AtlasTests.AnswersNeverDependOnResidencyOrCacheBudget` compares three runs and finds identical values, inputs and checksums:
  1. a 64 MiB cache;
  2. a 1-byte (starved) cache;
  3. a full unload before every query.
- `WritesRefreshEveryCachedLevelAndKind` interleaves six writes with queries of three fields at all four levels and with area statistics. Its warm cache answers exactly like unloading before every query, including change times.
- `ConservativeRefinementPreservesEachNativeCellsMean` checks the reported residency: refined after tactical sampling of a conserve field, loaded for an interpolated one, unloaded after an unload.

This re-proves the residency half of SOV-P02-T10 and SOV-P02-T16, which Phase 02 left as deviations. The client shows residency as an overlay and cache statistics in the HUD (T14). **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T08 — Generated base tiles with mutable deltas and nondestructive local refinement
- **Base:** an `IBaseSource` fills chunks deterministically from seed and generator version, and is never saved. Phase 03 has only a constant base.
- **Deltas:** stored fields hold sparse per-cell writes, each with the time it was written. A write that restores the base value is kept too, so every cell's change time is saved state.
- **Refinement:** finer levels are computed from native data and never modify it. A conserve field's refinement terms are cached per native chunk (resident state "refined").

Tests: `ARemoteUnloadedCoordinateKeepsItsIdentityAfterReloadAndRefinement`, `ConservativeRefinementPreservesEachNativeCellsMean` and `ConservativeRefinementStaysWithinTheFieldsRange`. **Deviation:** persistent tactical write-back to native data is Phase 12 and Phase 37 work, under ADR-0009's rules. **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T09 — Safe aggregation and consistent local sampling from macro fields
- **Coarse levels:** each coarse cell is the exact mean of the native cells inside the world (`CoarserLevelsSeeExactMeansOfNativeCells`, 16 × 16 local cells). A native cell partly outside the world counts by its part inside, so coarse values equal `Aggregate` at the world's edge too (`CoarseMeansWeightEdgeCellsByTheirPartInsideTheWorld`, a 40 km world of 156.25 regional cells).
- **Area statistics:** `Aggregate` weights each native cell by its overlap with the rectangle and the world, so mean × area is an exact total (`AreaStatisticsWeightCellsByOverlapSoTotalsAreExact`).
- **Finer levels:** conserve fields add the closed-form bilinear-mean correction, so each native cell's refined mean equals its value to 1e-12 on a curved profile. An interpolated field does not (`ConservativeRefinementPreservesEachNativeCellsMean`). Where the corrected surface would leave the field's range, a limiter blends it towards the cell value, which keeps both mean and range: an empty cell among full ones refines to 0, never below (`ConservativeRefinementStaysWithinTheFieldsRange`).
- **Categories:** refused at registration until majority-class aggregation exists, so no coarse level averages class indices (`CategoryFieldsAreRefusedUntilMajorityAggregationExists`; ADR-0009; Phase 04 implements it).

**Deviation:** a coarse sample's change time is the latest write in the native chunks involved. That can be later than the last change to the exact inputs, never earlier. Native, derived and refined samples are exact (T13). **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T10 — Authoritative storage versus recomputable caches
ADR-0009 "Storage, deltas and refinement" and "Chunks, residency and memory":

- **Saved:** stored overrides with their write times, and features (save format 2).
- **Never saved:** base data, which regenerates; derived fields; every cached chunk; the feature index.

Every cache entry is recomputable from base, overrides, clock and identity, and is invalidated by its sources' writes (T11). `AnswersNeverDependOnResidencyOrCacheBudget`, `WritesRefreshEveryCachedLevelAndKind` and `AtlasStateSurvivesSaveAndLoad` show caches never act as truth: a starved cache, a full unload, writes after caching, and a save–load all give the same answers and checksum. **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T11 — Dependency-driven invalidation
A write to a field invalidates cached chunks of that field, and of every transitive dependent, that intersect the changed area plus one native cell of the coarsest affected field. Features bump the `features` state version, which rebuilds their index.

- `AtlasTests.ChangesInvalidateOnlyDependentChunksNearThem`: painting scratch refreshes the nearby gradient, and a distant cached gradient chunk is reused (exactly one recomputation).
- `FeatureTests.ChangingALineRefreshesTheIndexItsQueriesUse`: removing a line is seen by the next query, and unchanged lines reuse the index.
- `WritesRefreshEveryCachedLevelAndKind` (T07) covers native, derived, aggregate and refined chunks after writes.
- `GradientSeesStepsBetweenUniformBaseChunks`: a derived chunk counts as uniform only when its neighbours share its value, so a step between two uniform base chunks has its gradient (a generated base can be uniform per chunk).

**Deviation:** rivers, roads and forests do not exist until their phases. The mechanism is shown with scratch → gradient and features → index; those phases declare `DependsOn` and get the same invalidation. **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.

## SOV-P03-T15 — Data migration strategy for new fields and legacy worlds
ADR-0009 "Migration" and ADR-0008:

- Save format 2 adds stored field overrides and features. The declared migration 1 → 2 adds empty sections.
- Integrity is checked on the original payload before migration.
- A new field needs no migration while it has no overrides, because base regenerates.
- Changing a field's saved meaning bumps its schema and needs a migration; until then the save is refused with the reason.

`SaveTests` covers:

- `AFormatOneSaveMigratesAndContinuesLikeTheUninterruptedRun`: a real Phase 02 save (`F-KERNEL` at day 20, format 1), migrated and continued with the fixture's journal, reaches the uninterrupted run's checksum.
- `FieldDataThisBuildCannotHonourIsRefused`: an unstored field, an unknown schema and a wrong level are refused.
- `AtlasStateSurvivesSaveAndLoad`, and the header tests for newer formats.

## SOV-P03-T16 — Benchmarks of queries, chunk changes, aggregation and memory
`sovereigns-headless bench` paints 40 seeded strokes (446 906 overrides) on a 100 km × 100 km world, warms up, then measures each case. CI runs it as a smoke check on Ubuntu with 5 000 samples, and records numbers without asserting them. `HeadlessTests.BenchMeasuresEveryAtlasCaseAndPrintsJson` covers the output.

Local Release run after the review fixes, macOS 27.0.1, 10 cores, 100 000 random points per case:

| Case | Samples/s | Chunk loads | Evictions | Allocated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Latitude, analytic | 32.1 M | 0 | 0 | 21 MiB |
| Scratch, local native | 90.0 k | 81 278 | 79 230 | 2.6 GiB |
| Scratch, regional aggregate | 1.32 M | 317 | 0 | 140 MiB |
| Scratch, macro aggregate | 1.51 M | 269 | 0 | 135 MiB |
| Scratch, tactical refined | 90.2 k | 81 278 | 79 230 | 2.6 GiB |
| Gradient, local derived | 36.3 k | 124 650 | 123 452 | 6.8 GiB |
| Scratch, local, 1 MiB cache (churn) | 184 k | 102 736 | 102 704 | 3.2 GiB |

- **Whole-world aggregate:** 39 062 500 local cells in 73 ms. Unpainted chunks take the uniform shortcut. The managed heap grew by at most 16 MiB while it ran (sampled every millisecond).
- **Memory:** the cache stays at its 64 MiB budget. The managed heap is 93 MB after the run, against 76 MB before.

**Reading:** random global access at local resolution churns the cache, because the whole world is 312 MB of local chunks. Allocation is therefore high (every miss allocates a 32 KiB chunk). Real access is localized, and aggregates over painted regions stay resident. The churn case beating the 64 MiB native case suggests garbage-collection pressure from large evicted arrays. Before the review, whole-world aggregation held every chunk it visited (about 300 MiB); it now holds one at a time. Pooling chunk arrays is a measured candidate for Phase 51. **Deviation:** there are no thresholds until budgets are agreed on reference hardware (QUALITY_BAR). **State:** Verified on merge of ⟨PR⟩.
