## Phase 05 — Drainage topology, rivers, lakes and base groundwater structure
**Depends on:** 04.  
**Outcome:** Water networks follow contours and have conserved, physically meaningful routing.  
**GDB:** §§4.4–4.8, 5.9–5.12, 32.10.

- [ ] **SOV-P05-T01** — Identify watershed boundaries, ridgelines and drainage divides from the generated elevation.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T02** — Resolve flatlands/depressions into explicit lake, wetland or endorheic basin semantics rather than mysterious water traps.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T03** — Compute connected downslope routing and ordered upstream/downstream catchment relationships.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T04** — Derive river-source potential from catchment, runoff/climate proxies and coherent flow accumulation.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T05** — Generate permanent and seasonal channel networks as explicit, durable spatial features.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T06** — Model confluences so tributary quantity and downstream discharge are coherently accumulated.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T07** — Produce physically plausible river gradients, channel widths, bank shapes and valley relationships.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T08** — Create lakes with basins, shoreline, inlet/outlet connectivity and water storage.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T09** — Represent groundwater reservoirs/recharge regions at the appropriate abstraction.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T10** — Represent floodplains, wetlands and estuarine constraints from actual local terrain.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T11** — Preserve river and lake feature identity across world/local/tactical detail levels.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T12** — Audit each catchment path to sea, valid lake outlet or an explicit closed-basin sink.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T13** — Assert that river channels don't flow uphill without a specifically modeled exception.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T14** — Handle seasonal watercourses without permanently painting them as full rivers.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T15** — Create water-system diagnostics: upstream area, flow direction, lake capacity, connected sinks and cross-border river paths.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T16** — Test worldgen seeds with low relief, mountainous terrain, broad floodplains, dry basins and coastal deltas.
- [ ] **SOV-P05-T17** — Confirm a selected river's geometry survives zoom/unload/reload and can later anchor a tactical crossing.

**Exit gate 05 — Checkpoint A:** An inspectable world has coherent elevation, catchments, lakes, connected rivers and stable feature identity at all spatial scales.
