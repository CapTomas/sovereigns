## Phase 15 — Settlement geography, habitation and useful sites
**Depends on:** 14.  
**Outcome:** Settlement locations arise from real environmental and transport advantages.  
**GDB:** §§4.9–4.11, 7, 10, 15.

- [ ] **SOV-P15-T01** — Define physical suitability inputs for water supply, agricultural land, defensibility and exposure.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T02** — Define access-to-resources, terrain buildability, local climate and natural hazard constraints.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T03** — Distinguish hamlets, villages, towns, ports, strongholds and other settlement footprints.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T04** — Generate initial settlement candidate locations using local sustainable resource access.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T05** — Avoid settlements in inaccessible steep rock, active river channels and unrecoverable hazard zones.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T06** — Allow exceptions with explicit causal foundations such as a mine or fortified pass.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T07** — Generate settlement footprints, paths and local land parcels tied to map coordinates.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T08** — Allocate surrounding farmland, pasture, woodland and other land use without inventing biomes.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T09** — Identify likely bridge, ford, harbor, ridge fortress, water mill and pass trading sites.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T10** — Derive local farmland catchments and accessibility rather than using polygon ownership as terrain.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T11** — Represent legal claims and actual settlement site occupancy as separate future-ready fields.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T12** — Keep construction and village extent persistent when terrain is revisited at tactical scale.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T13** — Provide founding, abandonment, destruction and rebuilding event states.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T14** — Build map overlay for settlement opportunity, growth limits and water access.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T15** — Test two settlements competing for finite nearby farmland and resources.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T16** — Test that deliberate upstream irrigation can affect downstream users later.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T17** — Validate navigation and settlement data after coasts, rivers or bridges change.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T18** — Generate comprehensible settlement-quality explanations suitable for player tooltips.
- [ ] **SOV-P15-T19** — Seed reference worlds with plausible villages, a city site, a mining site and a pass fortress.

**Exit gate 15:** Generated settlements have explainable physical reasons to exist and stable real footprints, including difficult trade-offs rather than identical generic cities.
