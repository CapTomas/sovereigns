## Phase 20 — Local markets, autonomous trade and economic price feedback
**Depends on:** 19, 17 and 18.  
**Outcome:** Private supply and demand connect production to consumption through prices and routes.  
**GDB:** §§10, 12–14, 25.2.

- [ ] **SOV-P20-T01** — Define local market inventories, demand, offers and measured scarcity separately from royal stores.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T02** — Define household and production-site purchasing demand from real consumption and inputs.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T03** — Compute local market prices with stable scarcity response and sufficient trading liquidity rules.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T04** — Represent merchants as aggregated rational-but-imperfect carriers of goods among accessible markets.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T05** — Calculate transport expenses, tariffs, timing and expected losses into trade opportunities.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T06** — Model trade as planned shipments whose goods are reserved and actually transported.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T07** — Support local transactions that do not need inter-settlement shipment entities.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T08** — Distinguish nominal wealth, goods inventory, productive equipment and physical resources.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T09** — Create market response delays and inventory buffers to prevent oscillation exploits.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T10** — Allow reasonable substitutes and emergency consumption adjustments when a commodity runs short.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T11** — Represent income distribution and purchasing power constraints on demand.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T12** — Model merchants preferring safer/profitable routes without knowing unrevealed enemy information.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T13** — Connect resource-site output to workshop inputs through market access, not shared kingdom magic.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T14** — Show price history, shortage origin, alternative suppliers and transport barriers.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T15** — Test wet-road closure causing grain price increases in an isolated mining town.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T16** — Test increasing milling capacity only helps when grain, labor and fuel/power are accessible.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T17** — Test an export boom attracting workers while raising a locally scarce input price.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T18** — Test negative-price, missing-supplier, zero-population and empty-market edge cases.
- [ ] **SOV-P20-T19** — Balance and profile 10, 100 and 1,000 interacting economic centers.

**Exit gate 20 — Checkpoint E — Trade works:** A mining town imports food and exports metal through real routes, responds to blockage by price and production changes, and never magically shares stockpiles.
