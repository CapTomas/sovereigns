## Phase 42 — Campaign completeness: objectives, losses, chronicles and strategic replayability
**Depends on:** 41 and generated history.  
**Outcome:** A complete campaign has meaningful beginnings, progression, setbacks and endings.  
**GDB:** §§1–2, 7.5, 24.6, 28, 31.

- [ ] **SOV-P42-T01** — Design faction selection based on actual generated geographic and institutional differences.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T02** — Provide summaries of starting strengths, constraints, rivals and strategic opportunities.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T03** — Implement campaign ambitions as optional milestones rather than forced sandbox termination.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T04** — Define attainable goals such as unifying a basin, securing trade passes or defending independence.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T05** — Implement lawful ending/defeat after actual collapse of recoverable sovereignty and forces.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T06** — Preserve vassal-play continuation and restoration where politically and physically possible.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T07** — Track founding, wars, battles, dynastic changes, disasters and infrastructure in campaign chronicle.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T08** — Produce location-specific history that references true world events and map coordinates.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T09** — Add era progress summaries without introducing an arbitrary global medieval technology level.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T10** — Create late-game strategic pressures from administration, logistics, factions and supply rather than cheats.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T11** — Ensure passive sandbox play is meaningful through dynamic trade, ecology and neighbor decisions.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T12** — Verify kingdom and world development can continue over centuries at practical speed.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T13** — Ensure abandoned settlements and destroyed infrastructure can later be restored through real projects.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T14** — Add decision reports that link high-level events to underlying physical/economic causes.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T15** — Provide history map replay distinguishing past legal borders from military control.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T16** — Generate multiple starting configurations with different economy, terrain and diplomacy challenges.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T17** — Test a small polity surviving by diplomacy despite much larger neighbors.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T18** — Test successful expansion followed by overreach/revolt without unavoidable artificial penalties.
- [ ] **SOV-P42-T19** — Test stable campaign save/load and chronology across several rulers and wars.

**Exit gate 42:** Players can complete meaningful long campaigns, lose fairly, continue after succession, pursue optional ambitions and see real historical consequences.
