## Phase 17 — Fields, crop seasons, livestock and food storage
**Depends on:** 16 and active climate/water/ecology.  
**Outcome:** Agriculture emerges from parcels and weather history; a seasonal food economy becomes functional.  
**GDB:** §§6.6–6.7, 11, 5.20, 31.3.

- [ ] **SOV-P17-T01** — Define crop species data: temperature range, growth thresholds, water tolerance, soil needs and use.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T02** — Define planting windows, growth stages, harvest windows, seed retention and growing-degree response.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T03** — Tie crop growth to soil history, solar heating, water stress, frost and farm management.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T04** — Track active cultivated area and human/animal labor availability per agricultural parcel.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T05** — Implement fallow/rotation and soil-nutrient depletion/renewal over seasonal timescales.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T06** — Model crop losses from flooding, drought, late frost and documented pest pressure.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T07** — Allow farmer decisions to select plausible crops from local suitability and available seed.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T08** — Create livestock aggregates with grazing, fodder, reproduction and winter feed requirements.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T09** — Represent feed consumption and manure/land pressure where gameplay-relevant.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T10** — Track harvest yields as actual grain/food inventory rather than abstract income.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T11** — Allocate food to households, seed reserves, animals, crown dues and marketable surplus.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T12** — Model spoilage, preservation and storage-capacity effects across months.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T13** — Represent famine pressure as inventory/access failure and household welfare, not one-shot event.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T14** — Allow basic grain-reserve policy hooks without demanding player micromanagement.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T15** — Compare hill farming, lowland farming, coastal districts and floodplain parcels.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T16** — Test removing farm workers during harvest and restoring them during winter.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T17** — Test a cold wet spring followed by summer drought and compare crop outcomes.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T18** — Test adequate imports avoiding starvation during a locally failed harvest.
- [ ] **SOV-P17-T19** — Add an inspectable annual crop record explaining yield by heat, water, soil and labor.

**Exit gate 17 — Checkpoint D — First people-food loop:** A year of weather causes believable crop growth, yields, food inventories and shortages; harvesting is a real event and food is neither created nor lost arbitrarily.
