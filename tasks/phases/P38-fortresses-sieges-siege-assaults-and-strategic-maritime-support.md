## Phase 38 — Fortresses, sieges, siege assaults and strategic maritime support
**Depends on:** 24 and 28–37.  
**Outcome:** Fortifications and blockade operations work as physical infrastructure with strategic constraints.  
**GDB:** §§15.4, 21.6, 23–24.

- [ ] **SOV-P38-T02** — Create defensible wall, gate, tower, ditch and tower/bailey geometry constrained by local terrain.

- [ ] **SOV-P38-T05** — Implement siege initiation by occupation/blockade of realistic access routes.

- [ ] **SOV-P38-T07** — Track attacker camp, supply lines, disease exposure and construction of siege works.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T08** — Support blockade, demand surrender, bombard, mining/engineering abstraction and assault orders.

- [ ] **SOV-P38-T12** — Allow defenders to maneuver, hold gates and make sorties under real combat rules.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T13** — Allow attackers to breach gates/walls through time- and equipment-dependent actions.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T14** — Prevent siege troops from passing through undamaged wall geometry.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T15** — Allow river/sea port supplies to bypass inland blockade where reachable and legally permitted.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T16** — Support strategic river craft, maritime trade ships and naval transport without requiring tactical naval battles.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T17** — Resolve ship travel and weather/navigation restrictions through ocean/river state at strategic scale.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T18** — Track fortress surrender terms, damage, confiscations and occupation separately from annexation.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T19** — Test an isolated pass castle surviving longer due to stored grain and secure spring.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T20** — Test relief through an open river port despite blocked roads.
- [ ] **SOV-P38-T21** — Test wall damage remains on future tactical map and affects trade/defense after siege.
