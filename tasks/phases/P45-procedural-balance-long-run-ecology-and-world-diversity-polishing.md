## Phase 45 — Procedural balance, long-run ecology and world diversity polishing
**Depends on:** 14, 40–44.  
**Outcome:** Random worlds remain geographically plausible, strategically distinct and economically survivable.  
**GDB:** §§3–7, 29, 31–32, 37.

- [ ] **SOV-P45-T01** — Establish worldgen quality scorecards for landform continuity, watersheds and realistic climate patterns.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T02** — Batch thousands of seeds at diagnostic resolution and flag pathological generation outcomes.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T03** — Measure plausible forest, grassland, wetland, alpine and dry-region distributions.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T04** — Evaluate population distribution and settlement density from food, water, trade and safety.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T05** — Evaluate starting-state kingdom power spread and avoid every world becoming a single superstate.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T06** — Validate mountain-pass frequency, river crossings, coastline trade and natural defensive terrain.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T07** — Check rain shadow and atmospheric seasonality across every climate band.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T08** — Check long-run water, soil, snow and ecological reservoirs do not drift unnaturally.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T09** — Review historical world development for viable agriculture, migration, institutions and towns.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T10** — Test alternate starting eras and map sizes without conflicting with saved historical events.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T11** — Create diversity metrics showing distinct campaigns rather than merely different landmass outlines.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T12** — Balance disaster extremes with discoverable causes, preparation opportunities and resilience.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T13** — Check yields, trade, taxation, war attrition and reconstruction under contrasting regional conditions.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T14** — Ensure rare climates remain physically coherent and tactically meaningful rather than gimmicks.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T15** — Adjust generation settings, not battle cheats, to correct fundamentally unfair starting geographies.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T16** — Check procedural history can generate distinct religions, cultures and political arrangements.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T17** — Audit worlds with sparse resources and limited farmland for viable trade or migration strategies.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T18** — Create documented outlier-review process and deterministically reproduce bad seeds.
- [ ] **SOV-P45-T19** — Collect playtester evaluations of interesting chokepoints, unexpected history and strategic novelty.

**Exit gate 45:** A broad seed suite yields distinctive playable worlds with understandable climate/ecological patterns and no systemic worldgen corruption.
