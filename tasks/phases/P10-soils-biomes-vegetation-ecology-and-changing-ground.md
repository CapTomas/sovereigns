## Phase 10 — Soils, biomes, vegetation, ecology and changing ground
**Depends on:** 09, geology and regional climate.  
**Outcome:** The world develops ecosystem state constrained by real land, heat and water.  
**GDB:** §§3, 4.6–4.9, 6, 11.

- [ ] **SOV-P10-T01** — Represent soil parent material, texture, depth, nutrient potential, drainage and organic matter separately.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T02** — Initialize soil distributions from geology, topographic position, sediment and climate history.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T03** — Derive soil fertility and agricultural constraints without fixed biome-tag bonuses.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T04** — Define vegetation functional groups and ecosystem tolerances as data rather than code constants.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T05** — Calculate suitability from temperature range, growing season, moisture, soil and disturbance.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T06** — Initialize plausible forests, scrub, grassland, wetlands, tundra, desert and alpine ecosystems.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T07** — Simulate seasonal leaf/ground cover and dormancy without replacing annual ecological growth.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T08** — Simulate vegetation growth, competition, mortality, succession and biomass accumulation at aggregate scale.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T09** — Represent woodland age, density, fuel, canopy exposure and available timber resources.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T10** — Represent wetlands through sustained hydrology rather than arbitrary painted marsh tiles.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T11** — Connect plant evapotranspiration and surface shade back into soil and heat/moisture fields.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T12** — Reflect vegetation effect on erosion, runoff and access where significant and affordable.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T13** — Track wildfire risk, ignition, spread and fuel depletion as environmental state transitions.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T14** — Represent ecological recovery after fire, harvest, deforestation and abandonment.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T15** — Support persistent human land-cover changes and realistic recovery timescales.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T16** — Represent wildlife/pasture productivity at appropriate aggregates without simulating each animal.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T17** — Build overlays for soil, vegetation suitability, biomass, disturbance and regeneration.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T18** — Test rain shadows, alpine treelines, dry valleys, wet depressions and long-term reforestation.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T19** — Validate no forest instantaneously appears solely because climate becomes favorable for one day.
- [ ] **SOV-P10-T20** — Confirm harvested timber decreases real biomass available for later production and affects battle cover.

**Exit gate 10:** Biomes are results of soil, heat, water and ecological history; removing a forest changes resources and cover and takes time to recover.
