## Phase 21 — Rights, holdings, taxes and crown-versus-private wealth
**Depends on:** 20 and population livelihoods.  
**Outcome:** The ruler governs an economy they do not directly own.  
**GDB:** §§9, 14, 16, 21.8–21.9.

- [ ] **SOV-P21-T01** — Define legal ownership of land, settlements, productive assets and government titles.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T02** — Represent crown land, noble holdings, communal rights, church or religious holdings and chartered towns.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T03** — Represent legal authority, actual administrative enforcement and military control distinctly.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T04** — Define recurring rents, produce dues, tolls, urban levies and extraordinary contributions.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T05** — Represent taxes payable as coins, goods, service or other modeled obligations.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T06** — Use actual taxable surplus and local institutional rights to calculate feasible collection.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T07** — Create treasury and crown store inventories distinct from household and private goods.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T08** — Define royal expenditure for government, soldiers, infrastructure and debt obligations.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T09** — Represent local resistance, corruption or collection friction without creating free tax revenue.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T10** — Define vassal service obligations and enforceable levies tied to political relationships.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T11** — Keep local economic entities producing and trading irrespective of which polity claims the land.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T12** — Model debt issuance, creditors, repayment and default consequences at a bounded level.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T13** — Support treasury collapse without instant destruction of every private business.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T14** — Expose expected versus collected taxes with reasons for shortfalls.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T15** — Represent privileges, exemptions and negotiated changes as legal history events.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T16** — Test taxes imposed during famine against stocks and legitimacy.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T17** — Test a rich commercial kingdom with poor crown finances and vice versa.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T18** — Test a lord refusing military service while continuing ordinary local commerce.
- [ ] **SOV-P21-T19** — Test an occupied district where physical control changes before legal ownership changes.

**Exit gate 21:** The player can levy funds and goods within actual institutional authority; royal wealth never equals all kingdom inventory and ownership remains legally coherent.
