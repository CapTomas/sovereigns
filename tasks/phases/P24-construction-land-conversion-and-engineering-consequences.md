## Phase 24 — Construction, land conversion and engineering consequences
**Depends on:** 23, 19 and resource economy.  
**Outcome:** Projects require resources, labor and geography; their physical changes persist.  
**GDB:** §§3.11, 6.8, 15, 21, 24.

- [ ] **SOV-P24-T01** — Define project types: road, bridge, mill, mine, granary, port, wall, fortification, irrigation and drainage.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T02** — Distinguish crown-built works, private organic expansion and ruler-encouraged development.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T03** — Validate legal rights and physical site suitability before a project begins.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T04** — Estimate materials, workers, expertise, route capacity and seasons from real context.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T05** — Reserve or procure goods without removing them from private inventory without a mechanism.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T06** — Track construction stages, delivery delays, workforce assignments and time consumption.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T07** — Build transport-linked construction supply using the same shipment model as ordinary trade.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T08** — Modify actual terrain and land-use layers for roads, walls, cuts, dams, drainage and clearance.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T09** — Modify catchment routing and flood exposure when terrain or hydraulic works legitimately change.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T10** — Invalidate affected topography, pathfinding, hydrology and visibility caches on physical edits.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T11** — Produce structures with state, damage, ownership, maintenance and repair requirements.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T12** — Implement maintenance, wear, abandonment and post-war reconstruction.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T13** — Allow bridge destruction to affect travel, supply, trade, army movement and battlefield view.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T14** — Allow reclamation/irrigation projects to change soil availability and production after realistic delays.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T15** — Permit settlement growth around completed infrastructure through autonomous incentives.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T16** — Expose project supply-chain, bottleneck, ETA and financial risk explanations.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T17** — Preserve construction stage during saves and after nearby battle loading.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T18** — Test fort placement on hill versus floodplain and explain feasibility differences.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T19** — Test a real river bridge built in campaign appearing in local tactical terrain.
- [ ] **SOV-P24-T20** — Test two projects competing for limited workers, timber and stone.

**Exit gate 24:** A player can commission a bridge or fortress, observe real delayed delivery and construction, and see resulting effects in water, economics, military paths and battle geography.
