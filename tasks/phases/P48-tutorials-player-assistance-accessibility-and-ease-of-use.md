## Phase 48 — Tutorials, player assistance, accessibility and ease-of-use
**Depends on:** 43–47.  
**Outcome:** Players can learn complex physics-driven strategy without external instruction.  
**GDB:** §§26.9–26.13, 28, 32.8.

- [ ] **SOV-P48-T01** — Create first-session guided world inspection highlighting elevation, wind, rivers and seasonal time.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T02** — Create concise campaign tutorial covering choice of kingdom, orders and seven-day progression.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T03** — Teach that land ownership, legal passage, military pressure and occupation are separate.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T04** — Teach road, bridge, granary and farm decisions through visible economic cause/effect.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T05** — Teach food stores, seasonal harvest and army labor consequences with natural scenarios.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T06** — Teach a simple allied-access movement that does not conquer an ally's territory.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T07** — Teach a hostile occupation requiring garrison and supply rather than a drawn map loop.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T08** — Teach military interception and what determines whether armies actually meet.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T09** — Create tactical tutorial for selecting, facing, moving and regrouping regiments.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T10** — Teach wind-affected archery and ground-condition-dependent cavalry movement visually.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T11** — Teach forests, hills, rivers and line-of-sight with contextual explanations.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T12** — Provide contextual diagnostics instead of forcing users to memorize hidden rules.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T13** — Implement configurable tooltips, notification density and information-panel presets.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T14** — Implement text size, contrast, key rebinding and reduced-motion settings.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T15** — Offer camera sensitivity, edge-scrolling, drag behavior and alternate input controls.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T16** — Support pausing on urgent alerts, while preserving correct chronological world progression.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T17** — Test tutorial flows with first-time players not familiar with grand strategy games.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T18** — Test accessibility features across map overlays, political layers, battle commands and alerts.
- [ ] **SOV-P48-T19** — Remove redundant prompts and excessive warnings identified in novice sessions.

**Exit gate 48:** A first-time player can navigate the world, govern an initial settlement, march legally and fight a battle while understanding cause and effect.
