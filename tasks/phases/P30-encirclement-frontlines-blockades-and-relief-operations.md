## Phase 30 — Encirclement, frontlines, blockades and relief operations
**Depends on:** 29 and supply networks.  
**Outcome:** Surrounding a place controls access rather than magically capturing enclosed map area.  
**GDB:** §§21.5–21.12, 23, 31.6, 32.5.

- [ ] **SOV-P30-T01** — Compute meaningful logistical connectivity among settlements, depots, armies and held crossings.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T02** — Derive threatened versus fully blocked transport connections from actual enemy presence and range.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T03** — Recognize enclosure through blocked routes and surrounding geography, not arbitrary drawn polygons.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T04** — Include mountain passes, ferries, navigable water, seasonal routes and coastal access.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T05** — Represent uncertain intelligence about routes behind fog of war.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T06** — Maintain supply, river transport and friendly passage for enclosed entities while routes remain open.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T07** — Represent garrison reserves, local food production, morale and response to isolation over time.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T08** — Make a surrounded village's decision to surrender depend on resistance, negotiation and relief prospects.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T09** — Allow an isolated but well-stocked fortress to resist long after its countryside is threatened.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T10** — Allow a small sortie or relief force to reopen a specific route without capturing an entire polygon.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T11** — Track the cost of maintaining a cordon using soldiers, supply and time.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T12** — Define frontline changes from deployment, patrol, attack, retreat, weather and road conditions.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T13** — Prevent encirclement status oscillating instantly from one frame of pathfinding noise.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T14** — Generate clearly explained alerts identifying which supply links were severed and by whom.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T15** — Show blocked access corridors, still-open escape paths, and probable encirclement zones.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T16** — Test paper.io-shaped cavalry loop with no garrison produces no annexation.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T17** — Test three blocked mountain passes isolating a valley without drawing a geometric ring.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T18** — Test lake/port evacuation defeating an incomplete inland blockade.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T19** — Test a relief army reopening one bridge and restoring delayed delivery.
- [ ] **SOV-P30-T20** — Test winter conditions naturally closing a secondary pass and completing a previously partial blockade.

**Exit gate 30:** Siege isolation follows physical supply connectivity and real time; a closed loop is neither sufficient nor necessary for encirclement.
