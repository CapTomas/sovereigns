## Phase 31 — Operational contact, battle invitations and tactical transition
**Depends on:** 27–30 and tactical terrain refinement.  
**Outcome:** An encounter is a consequence of positions, detection and orders, not shared administrative cells.  
**GDB:** §§20.4–20.8, 22.1–22.3, 24.2.

- [ ] **SOV-P31-T01** — Represent operational observation, reconnaissance, detection chance and stale sightings.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T02** — Calculate opposing force positions, headings, movement order and contact time within a strategic turn.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T03** — Model pursuit, avoidance, interception, ambush, bridge defense and enforced crossings.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T04** — Distinguish scouts skirmishing from a committed battle among full armies.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T05** — Allow a commander to halt, retreat, deploy defensively or seek engagement when feasible.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T06** — Select actual battlefield footprint from the contact location and suitable engagement scale.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T07** — Preserve approach directions, time of day, visibility and weather without rerolls.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T08** — Pass participating regiments, commanders, baggage and current conditions to tactical simulation.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T09** — Determine deployment limits from arrival directions and occupation of terrain before battle.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T10** — Allow neighboring forces to arrive as reinforcements only after plausible travel time.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T11** — Define when and how strategic time pauses for human tactical command.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T12** — Define tactical time spent and its return to the campaign's single world clock.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T13** — Handle units that decline a battle without automatic interception or forced combat.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T14** — Handle a bridge crossing blocked by a smaller enemy force with consistent tactical setup.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T15** — Handle separate attacks and multiple simultaneous encounters with deterministic chronological resolution.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T16** — Represent siege contexts as a related but distinct encounter type.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T17** — Create player notification with reliable versus rumored enemy strength distinctions.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T18** — Test encounter at dawn, storm arrival during approach and saved mid-turn battle interruption.
- [ ] **SOV-P31-T19** — Test nearby army outside reinforcement reach never appears as free troops.

**Exit gate 31:** Two armies meet only when movement, detection and engagement decisions produce contact; the resulting scene uses exact time, location, forces and geography.
