## Phase 33 — Melee contact, damage and meaningful battlefield interaction
**Depends on:** 32.  
**Outcome:** Combat outcomes follow physical positioning, weapons, fatigue and unit condition.  
**GDB:** §§19.4–19.6, 22.7, 22.9–22.10.

- [ ] **SOV-P33-T01** — Define melee engagement contacts using actual regiment geometry, facing and local position.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T02** — Model manpower engaged along a contact line without all soldiers fighting simultaneously.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T03** — Define weapon class, armor protection, shields, reach and tactical role through data-driven content.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T04** — Model attack/defense outcome under contact pressure, preparation, movement and cohesion.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T05** — Model charging as momentum and approach conditions, not a guaranteed impact multiplier.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T06** — Account for uphill/downhill approaches through geometry, fatigue and orientation.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T07** — Represent flank and rear contact through facing and committed formation geometry.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T08** — Track casualties, wounded and equipment loss under repeated contact.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T09** — Model unit crowding and inability to attack through another allied formation.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T10** — Handle fighting near steep terrain, marsh, river edge and building boundaries.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T11** — Prevent regiments from passing through enemy lines unless a modeled break actually occurs.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T12** — Allow ordered disengagement subject to contact and pursuit conditions.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T13** — Track reserve engagement and front-line replacement when formation depth permits.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T14** — Add melee performance diagnostics separating tactical rules from render effects.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T15** — Test cavalry charge against prepared infantry versus disrupted infantry on wet fields.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T16** — Test equal units on slope, open field, behind a narrow gate and in deep mud.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T17** — Test rear attacks and local breakthroughs without arbitrary teleport or collision resolution.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T18** — Ensure casualties are recorded at regiment level for campaign persistence.
- [ ] **SOV-P33-T19** — Compare multiple runs under identical seeds and orders for stable outcomes.

**Exit gate 33:** Unit roles, orientation, contact and real terrain change melee outcomes, and casualties persist as actual manpower losses.
