## Phase 40 — Knowledge diffusion, institutions and economic development over generations
**Depends on:** 22 and functioning economy/government.  
**Outcome:** Technology and institutional capabilities evolve plausibly through use, exchange and investment.  
**GDB:** §§7.2, 12.5, 18, 31.4.

- [ ] **SOV-P40-T01** — Define knowledge domains for agriculture, masonry, metalworking, navigation, logistics and military organization.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T02** — Define practical technology adoption separately from arbitrary universal unlock levels.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T03** — Let professions, cities, institutions and contact networks maintain and transmit knowledge.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T04** — Connect learned skills to trained labor, construction quality and manufacturing efficiency.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T05** — Model technology spread through migrants, trade, diplomacy and capture of knowledgeable people.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T06** — Allow deliberate patronage, schools, guilds and administrative reforms to accelerate diffusion.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T07** — Keep materials, access and workforce prerequisites even after new methods are known.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T08** — Create regional technological differences without permanently hardcoded civilization tiers.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T09** — Define era limits consistent with the medieval setting and material plausibility.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T10** — Make military-doctrine adoption require training and organizational change, not a one-turn unlock.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T11** — Model newly learned farming techniques changing yields only once adopted on actual fields.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T12** — Model shipbuilding, road engineering and fortress techniques affecting tangible project capability.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T13** — Represent knowledge loss or decline under destroyed institutions without instantaneous total amnesia.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T14** — Expose knowledge distribution, adoption timeline and opportunity indicators to the player.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T15** — Test a mining town improving its smelting only with available ore, fuel and skilled labor.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T16** — Test trade-network disruption slowing knowledge diffusion between neighboring cultures.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T17** — Test a culturally distinct state borrowing foreign tools without instantly copying its entire identity.
- [ ] **SOV-P40-T18** — Test AI states making rational long-term investments given geography and politics.

**Exit gate 40:** Civilization technology changes concrete world capabilities through diffusion, capital and skilled labor rather than magical tech-tree currency.
