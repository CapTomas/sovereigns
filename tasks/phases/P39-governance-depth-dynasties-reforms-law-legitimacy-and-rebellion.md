## Phase 39 — Governance depth: dynasties, reforms, law, legitimacy and rebellion
**Depends on:** 21, 23, 25 and military control.  
**Outcome:** Medieval politics mediates how much of an economy and territory the player can command.  
**GDB:** §§7–9, 14, 16–17, 21.8.

- [ ] **SOV-P39-T01** — Define ruler and succession rules while player identity remains the continuous kingdom.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T02** — Track dynasty histories, legitimacy and potential rival claimants at bounded character detail.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T03** — Represent council, estates, nobles, religious institutions and chartered town relationships.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T04** — Allow crown-domain policy changes subject to real ownership and governance powers.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T05** — Represent privileges, tax exemptions and service obligations as durable institutions.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T06** — Allow reforms to centralize or decentralize authority with time and political cost.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T07** — Represent petitions, concessions, disputes and political crises using actual economic or cultural causes.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T08** — Generate unrest from food, occupation, legitimacy, cultural relations and governance choices.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T09** — Model rebellions emerging from local organized factions, holdings and available manpower.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T10** — Allow civil conflict and splitting of states without deleting local productive economies.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T11** — Allow states to become vassals or tributaries without instant game-over if player sovereignty remains.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T12** — Track negotiations with lords for military service during unpopular wars.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T13** — Ensure councils and nobles do not act omnisciently about invisible enemy armies.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T14** — Allow regional governors to be delegated broad priorities and react to local constraints.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T15** — Define lawful confiscation, occupation confiscation and private rights as different actions.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T16** — Expose understandable government reports, tax obligations and political leverage.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T17** — Test an unpopular tax during a failed harvest escalating into refusal and rebellion.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T18** — Test succession changing dynastic control but preserving the player's chosen polity.
- [ ] **SOV-P39-T19** — Test a conquered region with retained legal privileges and resistant local lord.

**Exit gate 39:** Political institutions constrain authority while leaving kingdom play understandable and dynamic; rebellion, loyalty and succession have traceable causes.
