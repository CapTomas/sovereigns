## Phase 29 — Military presence, influence, occupation and territorial control
**Depends on:** 27–28 and political/legal access.  
**Outcome:** Military control emerges from presence, reachable nodes, local resistance and sustained force.  
**GDB:** §§21.1–21.5, 21.7–21.11, 32.5.

- [ ] **SOV-P29-T01** — Represent legal sovereignty, current governing authority, permission, occupation and claim separately.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T02** — Implement military influence as geographically reachable pressure, not a fixed circular radius.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T03** — Include road/river access, mountains, fortifications, weather and visibility in projected influence.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T04** — Separate transient threat, intermittent patrols, held routes and administered occupation.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T05** — Give units explicit Secure, March Through, Occupy, Raid, Patrol and Withdraw operational orders.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T06** — Model unfortified settlements submitting when facing credible force and lacking resistance options.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T07** — Let hostile forts and garrisons maintain contested influence even when bypassed.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T08** — Require credible detachments, installations or political cooperation to secure held territory.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T09** — Represent occupation supply, garrison burden, civilian compliance and possible unrest.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T10** — Remove unsustainable control gradually when the army departs without replacement authority.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T11** — Represent contested corridors and patchy rural control without painting full provinces at once.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T12** — Preserve allied/neutral land unchanged by lawful friendly transit.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T13** — Prevent a cavalry loop around a district from granting ownership or automatic surrender.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T14** — Represent control of strategic crossings, passes, market towns and supply nodes with concrete effects.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T15** — Compute local tax/trade availability under competing legal and physical control.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T16** — Give the player clear political-versus-military-control layers and uncertainty where intelligence is poor.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T17** — Define legal annexation through treaties, legitimate succession or sustained governance procedures.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T18** — Test a bypassed hostile fort exerting influence on nearby routes despite occupation elsewhere.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T19** — Test reoccupation after garrison removal, local rebellion and enemy counter-raid.
- [ ] **SOV-P29-T20** — Test ownership remaining unchanged throughout an allied march and return.

**Exit gate 29 — Checkpoint F — Territory responds:** Movement creates threat; sustained, supplied force secures places; law and institutions decide political title, and polygon loops never cause instant conquest.
