## Phase 35 — Battle morale, fatigue, cohesion, routing and victory
**Depends on:** 32–34.  
**Outcome:** Tactical command becomes a real battle rather than a collision demo.  
**GDB:** §§22.9, 22.13, 24, 19.5.

- [ ] **SOV-P35-T01** — Define morale and discipline distinct from stamina, cohesion and physical casualties.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T02** — Derive initial morale from training, leadership, pay, conditions and campaign history.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T03** — Model local morale pressure from losses, flank threat, commander danger and nearby routs.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T04** — Model fatigue from marching, heavy equipment, fighting, heat, cold and difficult ground.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T05** — Represent regrouping and recovery with realistic distance, safety and elapsed time.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T06** — Implement individual regiment wavering, routing and recoverable retreat.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T07** — Enable surrender and capture when escape or organized resistance becomes impossible.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T08** — Implement pursuit of routed units and protection by reserves or terrain.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T09** — Support commander death/injury/capture and consequential local command effects.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T10** — Keep army-wide morale as a result of constituent forces, not a global magic switch.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T11** — Implement combat objectives, withdrawal decision and mutually negotiated disengagement cases.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T12** — Define field-battle end conditions that account for rout, retreat, exhaustion and surrender.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T13** — Track tactical history events for later reports and campaign updates.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T14** — Build battle result summary for casualties, cohesion, commanders and territorial condition.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T15** — Test a fresh veteran regiment versus exhausted underpaid levy on favorable and unfavorable ground.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T16** — Test cascading rout under encirclement without deterministic unavoidable collapse.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T17** — Test depleted units withdrawing over an actual narrow bridge under pursuit.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T18** — Test attacker/defender retreat direction and regrouping linked to operational routes.
- [ ] **SOV-P35-T19** — Test battle speed/pause does not change the actual outcome for the same orders.

**Exit gate 35 — Checkpoint F — Playable battle:** A battle can be won through maneuver, shock, cohesion collapse or withdrawal and returns an explainable result rather than simply eliminating all soldiers.
