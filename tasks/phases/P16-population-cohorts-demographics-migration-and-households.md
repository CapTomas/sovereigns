## Phase 16 — Population cohorts, demographics, migration and households
**Depends on:** 15.  
**Outcome:** Places contain real people who work, consume, reproduce and migrate.  
**GDB:** §§7.1, 8, 10, 31.3.

- [ ] **SOV-P16-T01** — Define aggregated population cohorts by settlement and rural area, separating residents from workers.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T02** — Represent age structure, household dependence, employment capacity and human mortality.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T03** — Represent births, deaths, illness and survival as bounded demographic transitions.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T04** — Track household food needs, fuel demand, clothing, shelter and basic welfare over time.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T05** — Track population occupations and available/committed workers across economic activities.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T06** — Represent wealth and living standards at tractable cohort resolution.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T07** — Link disease and health risks to water quality, food access, crowding and climate as appropriate.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T08** — Model migration incentives from jobs, safety, food, land availability and culture.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T09** — Implement relocation through traversable routes and plausible travel time rather than teleportation.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T10** — Prevent double counting workers assigned to multiple sectors or recruited to an army.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T11** — Distinguish culture, religion, legal estate and political allegiance from a person count.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T12** — Create consistent population cohorts on founding, conquest, splitting and merging settlements.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T13** — Record population changes caused by flooding, famine, fires and abandoned settlements.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T14** — Add population inspections for dependents, labor distribution, food demand and movement.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T15** — Test a mining boom drawing migrants from a farming valley with consequential labor loss.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T16** — Test a poor harvest raising vulnerability but not instantaneously deleting inhabitants.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T17** — Test birth/death/migration population accounting and no negative age cohorts.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T18** — Test unloaded cities continue demographics in the same chronology as nearby active cities.
- [ ] **SOV-P16-T19** — Create reproducible demographic fixture reports spanning decades and multiple generations.

**Exit gate 16:** A settlement supports measurable population change and mobility without individual AI agents; labor, consumption and migration affect other systems.
