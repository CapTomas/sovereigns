## Phase 41 — Full strategic AI: autonomous polities, governors and military planning
**Depends on:** 23–40 and campaign observations.  
**Outcome:** AI states make informed, limited, intelligible economic and military choices.  
**GDB:** §§16–17, 20, 21, 25, 29.

- [ ] **SOV-P41-T01** — Define layered AI goals for polity survival, food security, defense, prosperity and political legitimacy.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T02** — Define budget, time and limited information constraints shared with the player kingdom.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T03** — Build strategic threat assessment from known armies, accessible terrain, treaties and supply.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T04** — Prioritize food supply and winter preparations using actual production forecasts.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T05** — Invest in roads, granaries, mines and fortifications where causal benefits are high.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T06** — Recruit and equip military forces from local manpower and real inventories.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T07** — Decide wars and peace based on interests, credible grievances, costs and military prospects.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T08** — Use reconnaissance reports rather than querying hidden world truth for hostile positions.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T09** — Plan operations through passes, roads and fords with seasonal movement limitations.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T10** — Use allied access and treaties before planning military passage across another state.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T11** — Identify enemy supply routes, defended nodes and encirclement opportunities.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T12** — Secure territory with garrisons and logistics rather than painted occupation polygons.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T13** — Evaluate siege, blockade, field battle, retreat and negotiation as alternative strategies.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T14** — Prioritize rest and resupply when campaigns run long or winter travel becomes costly.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T15** — Allow competing factions to have distinct risk preferences, personalities and institutions.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T16** — Delegate local economic operation and governance without processing each household as an agent.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T17** — Implement AI reasoning trace with goals, options, visible evidence and chosen action.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T18** — Calibrate reaction speed and difficulty without hidden free resources, movement or omniscience.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T19** — Run AI-only conflicts and verify no permanent deadlock, war-spam or runaway attrition spiral.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T20** — Test a river kingdom defending major crossings against a mountain polity's smaller forces.
- [ ] **SOV-P41-T21** — Test a threatened coastal state using commerce, mercenaries and diplomacy within actual means.

**Exit gate 41 — Checkpoint G — Independent world:** A seeded AI-only multi-kingdom campaign unfolds for decades with plausible economy, diplomacy, fronts, defense and varied war outcomes.
