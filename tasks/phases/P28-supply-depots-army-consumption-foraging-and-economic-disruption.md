## Phase 28 — Supply depots, army consumption, foraging and economic disruption
**Depends on:** 27 and commodity transport network.  
**Outcome:** Military logistics are an extension of ordinary production, storage and transportation.  
**GDB:** §§19.3, 19.7, 20, 23, 31.3.

- [ ] **SOV-P28-T01** — Define per-regiment provision, water, fodder, equipment, ammunition and pay consumption.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T02** — Define carried supply limits and baggage capacity based on actual transports and animals.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T03** — Connect army resupply to settlements, depots, warehouses, rivers, roads and allied access.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T04** — Track dispatch, in-transit stores, delivery and losses rather than kingdom-wide supply radii.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T05** — Provide supply forecast by army route, season, available inventory and required consumption.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T06** — Implement supply priority and procurement rules compatible with treasury and property rights.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T07** — Implement negotiated allied provisioning and purchases through treaty terms.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T08** — Distinguish permitted foraging, paid requisition, coerced requisition and outright looting.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T09** — Deduct foraged/requisitioned food and resources from local residents and land inventories.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T10** — Apply damaged land and civilian reaction consequences to raiding and prolonged occupation.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T11** — Simulate deterioration under prolonged shortage: fatigue, attrition, desertion and operational limits.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T12** — Represent weather-stranded convoys and blocked river deliveries.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T13** — Allow mobile depots, reserve stocks and controlled storage at strategic locations.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T14** — Allow a fleet/river convoy to supply a location only where navigation and permission permit.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T15** — Detect when forward armies outrun actual supply capacity and cannot be resupplied on schedule.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T16** — Show supply overlays for reachable stock, expected deficit, route capacity and hostile interdiction.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T17** — Test mountain army with short local growing season but a secure imported grain route.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T18** — Test blocked bridge and a delayed starvation outcome rather than instant effect.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T19** — Test siege army supply versus defenders behind walls, each using separate stores.
- [ ] **SOV-P28-T20** — Test supply/consumption mass balance across recruitment, movement, battle and retreat.

**Exit gate 28:** An army can be supplied, isolated or starved because of actual economic and geographical routes; shortages have delays and intelligible causes.
