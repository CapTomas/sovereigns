## Phase 26 — Recruitment, regiment identity, equipment and army organizations
**Depends on:** 23–25 and the economy.  
**Outcome:** Kingdoms field physically funded and socially grounded armies.  
**GDB:** §§18–19, 9.3, 11.6.

- [ ] **SOV-P26-T01** — Define regiment unit families, roles, training requirements, formation preferences and required equipment.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T02** — Define men-at-arms, levy, specialist and contract recruitment pathways from institutions and labor.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T03** — Derive recruitable manpower from actual population, eligibility and obligations.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T04** — Deduct serving personnel from productive labor and account for household economic consequences.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T05** — Derive equipment availability from real manufacturers, stores and purchases, not troop-card costs alone.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T06** — Model regiment size, officer structure, training, experience and condition.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T07** — Model pay, upkeep, contracts, equipment wear, ammunition and mounted-animal needs.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T08** — Represent professional troops and seasonal levies with differing service expectations.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T09** — Allow mustering, rallying, merging, splitting, reinforcement and demobilization over time.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T10** — Prevent recruits instantly appearing in distant armies without traveling through the world.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T11** — Assign commanders with meaningful organizational and operational abilities.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T12** — Model cavalry requirements from horses, fodder, training and equipment availability.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T13** — Define siege train and baggage elements with weight, road/bridge and transport needs.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T14** — Represent army camp location, current supplies, route and current marching posture.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T15** — Model morale/reliability baseline from training, recent service, pay and social conditions.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T16** — Implement regiment inspection of actual origin, workforce impact and equipment stocks.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T17** — Test emergency levies taking workers away just before a harvest.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T18** — Test recruitment blocked by a shortage of metal tools, weapons or horses.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T19** — Test vassal troop obligations, refusal and limited service expiry.
- [ ] **SOV-P26-T20** — Test disbanding survivors returns people to feasible locations and labor markets.

**Exit gate 26:** A kingdom can muster historically plausible regiments with real manpower, goods and institutional obligations; troops remain accountable after being raised.
