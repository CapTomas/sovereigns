## Phase 22 — Civilizations, cultures, religions and generated historical institutions
**Depends on:** 16–21 and complete physical world.  
**Outcome:** People form culturally distinct polities whose histories create real map state.  
**GDB:** §§4.10–4.11, 7–9, 31, 32.2.

- [ ] **SOV-P22-T01** — Define generated people groups independently of dynasties, kingdoms and current rulers.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T02** — Define culture, language, tradition and military-practice traits that change through history.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T03** — Generate place and polity names using consistent culture-specific naming schemes.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T04** — Initialize migration groups and early settlements from sustainable environmental opportunities.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T05** — Allow food surplus, trade and security to drive growth of settlements across early history.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T06** — Evolve local social practices under geography, technology, neighbors and political change.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T07** — Create religious traditions and organized institutions without relying on fantasy or supernatural power.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T08** — Model political consolidation from settlements, holdings, alliances and coercive capacity.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T09** — Create dynasties, office holders, councils and rulers as bounded individually relevant actors.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T10** — Track border changes, faction emergence, vassalage, fragmentation and rebellion through events.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T11** — Create wars, peace, trade and fortification consequences within historical simulation.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T12** — Build roads, towns and fortresses as genuine persistent changes caused by historical decisions.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T13** — Create history summaries from actual recorded events rather than generated lore disconnected from state.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T14** — Ensure cultures can spread or coexist without every conquest instantly changing identity.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T15** — Preserve legitimate geographic and economic constraints during accelerated centuries of genesis.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T16** — Audit that history does not consume more people, goods or territory than is available.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T17** — Generate interesting but not universally hostile starting political relationships.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T18** — Generate multiple plausible government structures from history and institutions.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T19** — Support choosing a surviving generated kingdom as the player polity.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T20** — Create chronicle UI/debug view tracing any castle to its historical founding conditions.
- [ ] **SOV-P22-T21** — Test multi-century history seeds without negative population or phantom settlements.

**Exit gate 22 — Checkpoint D/E — The world has history:** A seeded world opens with distinct societies, kingdoms, settlements, roads and conflicts whose origin can be traced to real preceding events.
