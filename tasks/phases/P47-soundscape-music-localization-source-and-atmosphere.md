## Phase 47 — Soundscape, music, localization source and atmosphere
**Depends on:** 46 and gameplay event model.  
**Outcome:** Audio and narrative feedback improve the world without becoming a substitute for physical state.  
**GDB:** §§1, 26, 27–28.

- [ ] **SOV-P47-T01** — Define separate audio identities for wilderness, settlements, campaign, siege and combat.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T02** — Create sound-event mapping from weather, landscape, work, army movement and battles.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T03** — Make rainfall intensity, wind exposure, rivers and forests influence environmental ambience.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T04** — Use current time and season to select appropriate ambient layers where meaningful.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T05** — Distinguish distant army movement, projectile fire, cavalry, melee and routing through audio cues.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T06** — Support procedural or layered sound playback at high entity counts without per-unit sound explosion.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T07** — Implement predictable audio priorities and listener controls for camera/zoom changes.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T08** — Choose music approach and commissioning/license rules consistent with final visual direction.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T09** — Create sufficient soundtrack for campaign calm, tension, combat and aftermath.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T10** — Build master, ambience, music, speech and effects volume controls.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T11** — Provide subtitles/text alternatives for important nonverbal alerts where gameplay-relevant.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T12** — Establish localizable string keys and pluralization from the beginning of polished UI text.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T13** — Create language-neutral generated names and historical event templates as appropriate.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T14** — Audit audio event calls for desync from actual physics and battle states.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T15** — Test repeated storms, crowded battles and rapid zoom changes for mixing/performance faults.
- [ ] **SOV-P47-T16** — Validate no core gameplay decision depends exclusively on audible information.

**Exit gate 47:** A full campaign and tactical encounter have coherent sound and interface text support without gameplay-critical inaccessible audio.
